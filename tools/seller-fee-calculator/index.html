How it is calculated

    
Buyer pays = item price + shipping charged. Fees = buyer pays × (sale % + processing % + ad %) + processing fixed fee + listing fee. Profit = buyer pays − fees − item cost − your shipping cost.

    
The break-even price and price for target profit solve the same equation for the item price, keeping your shipping charge fixed.

    
FAQ

    

      
Why aren't Etsy or eBay rates built in?
Fees change and depend on country, category and seller level. Use the numbers on your platform's current fee page so the result matches what you will actually be charged.

      
Are fees charged on shipping?
On most marketplaces, yes: transaction and payment fees apply to the total the buyer pays. If your platform differs, set shipping charged to 0 and add it to your payout manually.

      
What about taxes and refunds?
Not included. Sales tax or VAT collected by the marketplace usually is not your revenue; income tax depends on where you live.
