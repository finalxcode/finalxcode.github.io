Privacy Policy

Your inputs stay on your device. Images you load into the pattern generator and numbers you type into the calculators are processed by JavaScript in your browser. They are not uploaded. Calculator inputs are saved in your browser's local storage so they are still there next time; clear your browser data to remove them.

Analytics

This site may use Google Analytics to count page views and which tools are used (for example, that a pattern was downloaded). It does not receive your images or the numbers you enter. Google may set cookies; see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ad Settings.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

We collect only what is described on this page: optional analytics data, purchase records, and any email you send us. We use purchase records and email to deliver the product, answer support requests and process refunds. We do not send marketing email and we do not sell personal data.
