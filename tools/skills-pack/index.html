What you get

  • Topic Watch: checks RSS or Atom feeds and sitemaps you choose for new entries and turns them into a research shortlist. You add your own angle and verify the facts.
  • Ad Copy Check: flags phrases that may need evidence or revision, such as earnings promises and unsupported comparisons. It is an editorial prompt, not legal advice.
  • Markdown to WordPress: converts simple Markdown to an HTML preview, makes an optional cover image and prepares a WordPress draft with your own WP-CLI access.
  • Side Hustle Ledger: a CSV template and a weekly Markdown report to compare recorded time, cash in and cash out.
