How the quote is calculated

    
Quote = (real total hours × hourly rate + direct costs) × (1 + risk buffer) ÷ (1 − platform fee − tax), rounded up to the next 10 (or 50 above 1,000).

    
Real total hours = production hours + communication time + revision rounds × hours per round. The walk-away floor uses your minimum hourly rate and no buffer.

    
Example

    
6 production hours at 80/hour, 20% communication, 2 revision rounds of 1 hour, 30 of direct costs, 15% buffer and no platform fee gives 9.2 real hours, a quote of 890 and a floor of 490 at a minimum rate of 50/hour. A client budget of 600 sits between the two: negotiate by cutting scope, not your rate.

    
FAQ

    

      
How do I include platform fees in my quote?
Divide what you want to keep by (1 − fee rate). With a 20% fee, keeping 400 means quoting 400 ÷ 0.8 = 500. The calculator grosses up fee and tax together.

      
What is a walk-away floor?
The lowest price that still pays your minimum hourly rate with no buffer. Below it you are working for less than you decided you are worth.

      
How much communication time should I add?
Briefs, emails, calls and feedback usually take 10–20% of production time. Use your own time logs if you have them.

      
Should I charge a deposit?
A 30–50% deposit before starting is the simplest protection against unpaid work and clients who disappear.
