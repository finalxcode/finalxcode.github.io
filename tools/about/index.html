About

SideLedger Tools started as a side-project notebook: every time a side hustle needed a quick calculation or a pattern, the tool got built and published here for free.

Principles:

  • Everything runs in your browser. Images and numbers you enter are not sent to a server.
  • Arithmetic, not promises. The calculators show the math on your own numbers. They do not predict sales or income.
  • No sign-up. Open the page and use it.

Found a bug or want a tool? Open an issue on GitHub.
