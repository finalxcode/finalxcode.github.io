The break-even formula

    
Contribution per sale = price − cost per sale − fees. Break-even sales = fixed costs ÷ contribution per sale, rounded up. Sales for a target profit = (fixed costs + target profit) ÷ contribution per sale.

    
If the contribution is zero or negative, more sales will never get you back to even; the calculator flags this as not viable.

    
Example

    
A 39.90 product with 12 of cost per sale, no fees and 800 of startup costs earns 27.90 per sale, so it breaks even after 29 sales. At 25 minutes per sale that is about 66.96/hour, and with 300 minutes a week it takes about 2.4 weeks.

    
FAQ

    

      
What counts as a fixed cost?
Anything you pay no matter how many sales you make: equipment, software, a website, samples, an initial ad budget.

      
Where do marketplace fees go?
Percentage fees go in "Fees (% of price)". Flat per-order fees (listing fees, fixed processing fees) go in "Cost per sale".

      
Why ask for minutes per sale?
Time is a cost. Dividing profit per sale by the time each sale takes gives your real hourly rate, which is often the number that decides whether a side hustle is worth it.
